GTable Calculator

GTable Calculator is a Google Docs™ add-on that allows users to perform calculations on values in selected Google Docs™ table cells. Supported operations include addition, multiplication, average, count, maximum, minimum, and standard deviation. The add-on also includes a regular calculator.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how learnin60seconds.com applications collect, use, share, and retain information.

Word Cloud Generator: Google User Data

Word Cloud Generator may access text from the currently open Google Docs™ document, the signed-in user's Google account email address or user identifier, and export-credit status for optional high-resolution exports. It does not request broad Google Drive™ access and does not browse, list, or access files in the user's Google Drive™.

Document text is used only to generate the word cloud requested by the user. The user's email address or identifier is used only to associate high-resolution export access or credits with the correct user. Export-credit information is used only to check and record export access.

Word Cloud Generator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers, or use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Pixabay – Free Images: Data We Process

Search terms and selected filters are transmitted to our backend and Pixabay to return image results. Search results may be cached temporarily for performance and abuse prevention.

The current Google Docs™ document is accessed only as needed to insert images selected by the user and their source links. The add-on does not browse, list, or access unrelated files in the user's Google Drive™.

Google OpenID identity tokens are transmitted securely to our backend only for Pixabay Pro access verification and restoration. We do not store raw identity tokens, Google email addresses, or Google profile information. We store only a salted hash derived from the verified Google subject identifier to associate Pro access with the purchaser.

GTable Calculator: Google User Data

When the user requests a calculation, GTable Calculator accesses the currently open Google Docs™ document and selected table-cell values only as necessary to perform that calculation and, when requested, insert the result. It may also receive basic Google Account information, such as the user's email address, account identifier, and basic profile information, as permitted through Google's authorization process.

GTable Calculator does not browse, list, or access unrelated files in the user's Google Drive™. Document values are used only for calculations requested by the user. Basic account information is used only as necessary to authorize, operate, secure, or support the add-on.

GTable Calculator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers or information resellers, or use it to develop, improve, or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. It does not maintain a separate database containing users' Google Docs™ content or selected table-cell values.
